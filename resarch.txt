You do not strictly need Jev for V1.

ChatGPT/LLMs reason and generate plans. Jev makes fast, bounded decisions.

control plane

durable jobs
+
reconciliation
+
resource ownership
+
disk safety
+

+
backup correctness
+
explicit failure states
+
honest threat model
Your DeployMind should adopt those ideas and then extend them to the entire VPS/application lifecycle.

And there is one particularly important lesson from your own repo: your note.todo is already pointing at several of the right pain points—domain binding, local-only deployment, network/volume management, complete deletion, and Jev—but those should now stop being informal TODOs and become formal product requirements and acceptance tests.

At this point, I would treat the current main branch as architecture/prototype stage, not production-ready. The product idea is strong; the next job is to close the gap between the architecture document and the actual executable control plane.
